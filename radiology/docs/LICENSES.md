## Notes on the CT and MRI cases
- **TCIA Data Usage Policy** applies to UPENN-GBM, Lung-PET-CT-Dx and HCC-TACE-Seg. Its terms: do not identify or contact participants and do not generate facial images or comparable representations that could identify them. A site that mirrors TCIA data must give attribution, link to the policy and require downstream users to do the same. Every TCIA case's attribution ends with the sentence "TCIA Data Usage Policy: https://www.cancerimagingarchive.net/data-usage-policies-and-restrictions/". The owner should read the policy once before publishing.
- **Medical Segmentation Decathlon** copies are adaptations, so the manifest names the license as CC BY-SA 4.0 and the change note says the share-alike applies.
- **Faces.** Every head volume was viewed on a mid-sagittal and an orbit-level axial slice of the packed data. The TotalSegmentator volume has its facial soft tissue blurred in the source. OpenNeuro T1w and T2w carry the dataset's pydeface cut. UPENN-GBM had eyelid and nose contour left, so a box in front of the eyes was set to zero (see the case's change note).
- **No header fields.** Names, IDs, dates, institutions and scanner fields never leave the scratch folder. Packed files hold voxels only, and the manifest holds dataset-level text.
- **Not used.** ISLES 2022 (Zenodo 7960856) says CC BY 4.0 on its record page, but the LICENSE file inside its zip forbids redistribution without written agreement. FUMPE Patient32 has an empty ground-truth mask, so it is not a verified embolism case.
- **Headers can be wrong.** The NIfTI header of MSD Task02 Heart claims axes that do not match the anatomy. That case was rebuilt from the anatomy. Check orientation on the anatomy for every new volume, not on the header.

## Notes on the cath lab cases
- **De-identification.** None of the three sources states how it was anonymised. The ARCADE and CADICA files checked carry no text chunks, and the frames viewed carry no burned-in text. The IVUS clip carries two scanner overlays on every frame ("P:0.5" and "8.2 mm, 1 mm/div") and no name, id, date or hospital.
- **Projections.** Neither ARCADE nor CADICA states a projection angle, so no case names one and no image is captioned with one. CADICA lists videos only as LCA, LCA2 or RCA.
- **Labels are the datasets' own.** ARCADE polygons are partial (visible branches are sometimes unlabelled) and the page says so. CADICA boxes are on key frames only and the band names are not defined in the files.
- **Not used.** Wikimedia Commons "IVUS of CAD.png" (CC BY-SA 2.5): the author is only "original uploader", provenance and consent are not stated, and both panels carry a burned-in exam date. CADICA p12_v28: its list is LCA2, which the dataset does not define, a large catheter loop hides the proximal vessel, and its one box shows no narrowing.
